WHY OIL UNDERCOATING?

Protection that reaches the places rust begins.

Corrosion can begin in areas that are difficult to inspect or clean. Oil-based rust protection is designed to penetrate those vulnerable spaces.

Penetrating Protection

Creeping oil-based coatings can migrate into seams, joints, welds, and enclosed areas that are difficult to reach with conventional surface coatings.

Moisture Defense

Oil-based coatings help create a protective barrier between metal and the moisture, salt, and oxygen that contribute to corrosion.

Flexible Coverage

Unlike rigid coatings that may crack or trap moisture if compromised, suitable oil-based products remain flexible and can be reapplied as part of routine vehicle maintenance.

BUILT FOR NEW ENGLAND

Winter roads are hard on exposed metal.

New Hampshire roads see snow, freezing temperatures, road salt, and liquid deicing treatments. Salt-laden moisture can collect around vehicle underbodies, suspension components, seams, and cavities.

Regular inspections, cleaning, and appropriate corrosion-protection treatments can help reduce exposure and support long-term vehicle care.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions. Straight answers.

How does oil undercoating work?

Oil-based undercoating creates a protective film on suitable metal surfaces. Penetrating formulas can creep into seams and cavities, helping limit contact with moisture and road salt.

Can oil undercoating be applied to an older vehicle?

Some penetrating oil products are intended for vehicles with existing surface corrosion. Significant rust, structural deterioration, and loose material should be assessed before treatment. Undercoating does not repair damaged metal.

How often should undercoating be reapplied?

The appropriate interval depends on the product, driving conditions, and vehicle condition. Some oil-based systems recommend annual maintenance. Always follow the specific product guidance.

Is oil undercoating the same as rubberized undercoating?

No. Oil-based coatings are generally designed to remain flexible and penetrate vulnerable areas. Rubberized coatings form a different type of film and can require different preparation and maintenance.

Will undercoating eliminate rust permanently?

No corrosion-protection treatment can guarantee that rust will never develop. Good coverage, correct application, cleaning, and maintenance can help reduce corrosion risk.
